|---|---|
| “评估这批产品的身份和发育阶段”；necessary inputs missing | Load product-assessment, identify the consequential input gap; do not invent roles/windows. |
| Approved assessment has eligible P0-03 check and blocked P0-04 | Choose the useful supplied P0-03 option; do not transfer P0-04's blocker. |
| Approved report scope, graph_required and P0-09 option supplied | Composed reporting guidance prepares the graph, then the current explanation and explicit report handoff. |
| Approved scope includes graft assessment | Composed graft guidance preserves independent post-transplant evidence; no pre-transplant backfill. |
| “只解释已有结果，别运行” | Explain the current evidence; do not prepare a new plan or publish a report. |

These are behavioral cases. Execution, evidence admission and consent remain server-owned.
