## Research question and evidence boundary

The engineering question is whether a researcher can follow independent
cell-state/product axes, original denominators, missingness and exact
source/graph versions through a bounded approved check–evidence loop.

The retained upstream synthetic chain used one four-cell case. Ten shadow
developmental records in one family, including 2/4 = 0.5, remained immutable
across source-bound process checking, two-domain sufficiency, canonical append
and a second graph query. Five process measurements were genuinely
null/unavailable; graph version changed 1 → 2 and open requirements 11 → 12.
The stop was `no_discriminating_check`, with unchanged-input and
missing-claim-binding reasons. This was not real-product or scientific
qualification.

The interface preserves these distinctions. There is no score, ranking,
automatic family classifier, newly reviewed resource or scientific release.
Review of the 25 source-state cards precedes applicable product-role/window
approval and any signed FreezeGate. Program applicability and biological-unit
attestation remain independent gates.
