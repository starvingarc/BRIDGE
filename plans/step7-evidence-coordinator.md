- Composition is conditional on its selected DataView and declared denominator.
  Exclusive partitions retain unresolved mass; overlapping programs are
  independent axes, not a categorical simplex.
- Reference support, classifier scores, product roles and scientific qualification
  remain distinct. Method agreement is not a normalized posterior or numerical
  information gain.
- An action is admitted only inside the intersection of approved scope, current
  deterministic eligibility and remaining finite budget. Its exact request and
  authorization are persisted before execution.
- Hypotheses cite existing evidence, a competing explanation and a discriminating
  check; they do not assign measurements, evidence tiers or review status.
- Only verified tool outputs advance canonical graph evidence. Repeated queries
  or unchanged requests do not create new biological evidence.
- Evidence completion, exhausted budget, absent prerequisites, changed inputs and
  interruption remain distinct. The current scope has no applicable whole-question
  completion contract; a model completion proposal remains
  `completion_contract_unavailable`.
- All seven process families remain visible independently. Exploratory S/G2M
  supports cell-cycle description only; an unreviewed name mapping cannot fill
  the other axes. Local evidence bindings remain separate from provider context.
