## 提示词

Edit this scientific category icon into an extremely minimal monochrome line pictogram. Pure opaque white background. Medium gray (#777777) uniform bold strokes, white interiors. Flat 2D. Absolutely no color, no gradient, no shading, no 3D, no texture, no shadow, no text. Square canvas, centered, large clear form occupying 80% of canvas. Designed to remain legible at 40px. Keep only the essential identifying structure, dramatically reduce details. This is a category symbol, not an exact chemical structure.

### molecule

Show only two simple six-sided rings joined by a short angular connecting line. Omit all internal double-bond strokes and atom labels.

### protein

Show a compact folded protein as just three broad beta-sheet ribbon arrows joined by two smooth looping curves. Simple single gray outline, white ribbon interiors. Avoid crossings and secondary contour lines wherever possible.

### glycan

Show just three six-sided sugar rings in a short unbranched chain connected by short gray lines. Remove every pendant dot, substituent, and internal detail.

### supplement

Show just the outline of a squat laboratory reagent bottle, a simple rectangular cap, one horizontal liquid level, and three small circles below the liquid level. No label, cap ridges, perspective, reflections or extra contours.
